    Generative models on MNIST

    The autoencoder and the variational models reconstruct. Fashion is the easier sample domain, because a soft decoder still reads as clothing. The RealNVP digits run lowers bits per dimension and then flattens, and its inverse samples are still stroke fragments. Fashion RealNVP was trained for 20 epochs on grayscale images: test bits per dimension fall to 3.263, prior samples stay soft garment blobs, and reconstructions of real clothes stay sharp. Both vanilla GANs collapse to one shape. Conditioning on the class label brings sample spread in line with real images on digits and on Fashion. PatchGAN separates garments and still repeats one digit glyph. Feature matching keeps the class rows, and its generator loss sits above BCE because of the feature term. The progressive GAN grows from 4×4 to 32×32, and the last grid is one gray field.
